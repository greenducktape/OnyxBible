# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-06-21_

Onyx Bible is designed to be private by default.

## What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking
of any kind. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information.

## Where your data lives

Your handwritten notes, your printed Bibles, your reading-plan progress, and
your settings are stored **only on your device**, in the app's private storage.
They are never uploaded anywhere. If you uninstall the app or wipe the device,
this data is removed with it, so keep your own backup if it matters to you.

## Network use

The app works fully offline. The only time it uses the network is **optional**:
if you choose a translation that is not bundled with the app, it fetches that
translation's text from a public scripture API. Bundled translations and all
reading-plan data require no network access.

## Children's privacy

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the project's open-source
repository: https://github.com/greenducktape/OnyxBible
